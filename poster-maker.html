Social Media Poster & Banner Design Complete Guide

Aaj ke digital daur me social media par apne brand, channel, ya business ko promote karne ke liye ek achha visual banner zaroori hai. Hamara Free Poster Maker Tool aapko bina kisi Photoshop ya graphics knowledge ke HD banners banane ki azadi deta hai.

1. Correct Canvas Dimensions Chuniye

Har platform ka aspect ratio alag hota hai. Instagram Post ke liye Square (1:1) select karein, YouTube Thumbnails ya Banners ke liye Landscape (16:9), aur Instagram Stories / Shorts Promotion ke liye Vertical (9:16) preset chuniye.

2. High-Contrast Text Color Combination

Hamesha dark background par light colors (Yellow, White, Cyan) ka use karein. Hamare tool me {curly brackets} me likhe gaye text par automatic color highlight effect milta hai jo viewer ka dhyan turant aakarshit karta hai.

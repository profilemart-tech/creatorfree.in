Fresher Resume Ke Liye Career Objective Likhne Ka Guide

Jab aapke paas past work experience nahi hota, tab recruiter aapke resume ke **Career Objective** se yeh samajhta hai ki aapki motivation, skills, aur company me interest kitna hai.

Objective Copy Karne Ke Baad 3 Cheezein Zaroor Badlein:

  • Degree Name: Apni qualification (e.g., B.Tech, B.Com, BA, MBA, Diploma) sahi jagah replace karein.
  • Key Skills: Job requirement ke hisab se 2 main technical ya soft skills update karein.
  • Target Designation: Company ke job title (e.g., Junior Developer, HR Executive) ko exact matching text se badlein.

1. YouTube Thumbnail Standard Dimensions & Format

  • Recommended Resolution: 1280 x 720 pixels (Minimum 640px width)
  • 1080p Full HD Aspect Ratio: 16:9 widescreen ratio
  • File Format: JPG, PNG, ya WEBP
  • File Size Limit: Under 2MB limit for official YouTube video upload

2. 4 Secrets of High-CTR Viral Thumbnails

🔥 1. Sirf 3-4 Words Ka Bold Headline Text

Thumbnail par poori kahani mat likhein. Sirf 3-4 bold, impactful words likhein (jaise "SACH JAAN LO!" ya "DON'T BUY THIS!"). Fonts mein Teko, Bebas Neue, ya Anton ka use karein.

👤 2. High-Emotion Face Cutout

Insaan ke chehre aur emotions (shocked, happy, curious) dekh kar audience turant click karti hai. MediaPipe AI background removal se apni photo ka cutout lekar thumbnail par lagayein.

🎨 3. High Contrast Color Combo

Dark background (Dark Navy/Black) par Bright Yellow (`#fbbf24`), Bright Red, ya Electric Cyan text badge use karein taaki mobile screen par saaf dikhe.

✨ 4. Text Behind Subject (3D Depth Effect)

Text ko apni photo cutout ke peeche layer arrange karke lagayein. Yeh visual 3D effect thumbnail ko professional creator look deta hai.

A4 Paper Par Passport Size Photo Kaise Banaye Aur Print Karein? (DOP Date Stamp Guide)

By CreatorsFree Team • Updated August 2026

School admit cards, PAN card applications, passport renewal, aur sarkari exam forms (SSC, UPSC, NTA) ke liye 3.5 x 4.5 cm passport size photo ki zarurat hoti hai. Aksar hum studio ja kar ₹100-₹150 kharch karte hain ya Photoshop ki complex settings se pareshan hote hain.

Is guide mein hum aapko batayenge ki ghar baithe apne mobile ya PC se A4 paper sheet par 8, 16, ya 32 passport size photos zero paper waste ke saath kaise arrange aur print kar sakte hain.

1. Indian Passport Photo Size Specifications

Bharat mein zyada tar official documents aur school admit cards ke liye yeh standard dimensions follow kiye jaate hain:

  • Indian Passport Size: Width 3.5 cm x Height 4.5 cm (35mm x 45mm)
  • Aspect Ratio: 7:9 vertical ratio
  • Background Color: Plain White ya light Off-White background
  • Resolution: High-definition 300 DPI for crisp printing
  • DOP (Date of Photo): Admit cards ke liye student name aur photo lene ki taareekh Niche white box mein likhna

2. Step-by-Step: A4 Paper Par Photo Print Karne Ka Tarika

  1. Photo Upload Karein: Sabse pehle apne mobile ya PC se apni clear photo select karein.
  2. 1-Click AI Background Remove: Agar aapki photo ke peeche deewar ya messy background hai, toh CreatorsFree Passport Photo Tool mein Remove Background par click karke use white ya official blue mein badlein.
  3. Student Name & DOP Add Karein: Text & Date Stamp option mein apna naam aur Date of Photo (jaise DOP: 08/08/2026) add karein.
  4. A4 Layout Select Karein: 8 copies (Fast print), 16 copies, ya 32 copies grid select karein. Tool automatically A4 sheet ke top par photos arrange kar dega taaki baaki ka paper waste na ho.
  5. Scissors Cut Lines Enable Karein: Cut lines toggle karne se photo print hone ke baad scissor se sidha katne mein aasani hoti hai.
  6. Print Karein: Print button par click karein aur printer dialog mein Paper Size: A4 aur Margins: None/Default set karke print nikaalein.

💡 Printing Pro Tip for Home Color Printers

Agar aap regular Inkjet printer (Epson, Canon, HP) use kar rahe hain, toh standard Glossy Photo Paper (180 GSM - 210 GSM) ka istemal karein. Printer settings mein Paper Type: Glossy Photo Paper aur Quality: High select karein taaki studio-quality photo mile!

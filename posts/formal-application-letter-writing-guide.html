1. Formal Application Letter Ka Standard Structure

  1. To / Sewa Mein (सेवा में): Receiver ka pad/designation aur address (e.g. To, The Branch Manager, State Bank of India...)
  2. Date (दिनांक): Letter bhejne ki taareekh.
  3. Subject / Vishay (विषय): Letter likhne kam mukhya kaaran (e.g. Subject: Application for issue of new ATM Card).
  4. Salutation / Sambhodhan (महोदय/महोदया): Respected Sir / Madam.
  5. Body Paragraph / Mukhya Bhaag: Apni samasya ya nivedan ka spasht vivaran.
  6. Closing & Signature (भवदीय/आपका विश्वासी): Aapka Naam, Mobile Number, Account Number / Roll Number.

2. Top 5 Most Common Application Templates

  • Bank Application: Passbook Issue, ATM Card Re-issue, Cheque Book request, Account Closure.
  • School & College Leave Application: Sick Leave, Urgent Work Leave, Character Certificate (CC), Transfer Certificate (TC).
  • Police Station Complaint: Lost Mobile Phone, Lost Documents (Aadhaar/PAN), Missing Vehicles.
  • Electricity Board Application: Meter Change Request, Wrong Bill Correction.
  • Job Leave Application: Office Casual Leave, Earned Leave Application.

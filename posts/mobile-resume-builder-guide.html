1. Indian Resume Ke Essential Sections

  • Contact Info: Naam, Mobile (+91), Professional Email, City/State.
  • Career Objective: Freshers ke liye 2-line clear objective statement.
  • Education Qualification Table: Degree, School/College, Board/University, Passing Year, aur Percentage/CGPA.
  • Technical & Soft Skills: Job profile se related key skills.
  • Work Experience / Projects: Freshers ke liye academic projects aur experienced ke liye job roles.
  • Indian Declaration Line: "I hereby declare that all information provided above is true..." along with Date, Place, and Signature.
